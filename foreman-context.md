# The Foreman — Product Knowledge

## What it is
The Foreman is a free WhatsApp assistant for UK sole traders and small business owners. It runs entirely through WhatsApp — no app to download, no dashboard, no subscription.

## Who it's for
Any sole trader or small business owner who wants to manage their admin from their phone — plumbers, electricians, builders, decorators, gardeners, cleaners, and anyone else who works for themselves.

## What it costs
The Foreman is completely free to use.

## How to get started
Just text this WhatsApp number. The Foreman will ask for your business name and you're good to go. No sign-up form, no website, no app to download.

## What it can do
- **Quotes** — tell The Foreman who the job is for, what the work is, and the price. It generates a professional PDF quote you can send straight to your customer.
- **Invoices** — same deal. The Foreman builds the invoice and gives you a PDF to share. (Invoices are created here — you send them to your customer yourself from your own WhatsApp.)
- **Chase payments** — ask The Foreman to chase a customer and it'll draft a polite payment reminder for you to copy and send.
- **Track who's paid and who hasn't** — say "unpaid" to see all outstanding invoices, or ask about a specific customer.
- **Earnings reports** — ask how much you've made this month, this week, this year, or any time period.
- **Payment stats** — see your average payment time, quote conversion rate, and an overall financial summary.
- **Job tracking** — view all open jobs, pull up the details on any job, mark jobs as paid or complete, add notes.

## What it doesn't do
- The Foreman never messages your customers directly. It drafts the copy and PDFs; you forward them from your own WhatsApp.
- It doesn't give tax or legal advice.
- It doesn't connect to accounting software like Xero or QuickBooks.

## How it works
Everything happens through WhatsApp. Just text The Foreman in plain English — "quote for Mrs Smith, boiler service, £350" or "chase Darren about his invoice" — and it handles the rest. No commands to memorise.
